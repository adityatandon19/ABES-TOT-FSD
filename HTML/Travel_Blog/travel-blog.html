Exploring Paris

Paris, the city of lights, is known for its art, fashion, and culture. Highlights include the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, and charming cafes.

  • Best time to visit: April to June, October to early November
  • Must-try food: Croissants, Escargot, Macarons
  • Popular activities: Seine River Cruise, Visit Montmartre, Explore Le Marais

Discovering Tokyo

Tokyo is a bustling metropolis blending the ultramodern and the traditional, from neon-lit skyscrapers to historic temples. Key attractions include Shibuya Crossing, Senso-ji Temple, and Tsukiji Market.

  • Best time to visit: March to May, October to November
  • Must-try food: Sushi, Ramen, Tempura
  • Popular activities: Visit Akihabara, Explore Harajuku, Day trip to Mt. Fuji

Experiencing Budapest

Budapest, the capital of Hungary, is known for its stunning architecture, thermal baths, and vibrant nightlife. Don't miss the Buda Castle, Parliament Building, and Széchenyi Thermal Bath.

  • Best time to visit: March to May, September to November
  • Must-try food: Goulash, Langos, Chimney Cake
  • Popular activities: Cruise on the Danube, Explore ruin pubs, Visit Fisherman's Bastion
