Safety Mechanisms in Metro / Subway Systems

Metro systems are safe, efficient, and reliable, but their safety depends on public cooperation. Following rules ensures smooth operation and protects lives.

Do's (Follow These Safety Rules)

  • Stand behind the yellow line on the platform
  • Allow passengers to exit before boarding
  • Keep your belongings close to you
  • Use handrails while standing

Dont's (Avoid These Actions)

  • Do not lean on the doors
  • Avoid blocking the doors while boarding or alighting
  • Do not eat or drink inside the metro
  • Avoid loud conversations or playing music without headphones

General Information

India's metro systems are designed with advanced safety mechanisms that comply with international urban transport standards
